Histology 205 User Guide

Quick cheat-sheet for daily routine and bi-weekly audits

1. The Core Rule: Stock (Sealed) vs. In-Use (Open)

• Stock (Sealed): Bottles stored in flammables, acid cabinets, or 4°C refrigerators.
• In-Use (Open): The active bottle currently on the special staining bench or autostainer.
• Total = Stock + In-Use.

2. Your Daily Bench Actions

⚡ Open New Bottle

When you take a sealed bottle from the cabinet to the bench. It decreases Stock by 1 and increases In-Use by 1. If total drops to or below MIN QTY, it flags red automatically.

✓ Finish Bottle

When an in-use bottle runs dry. You can either discard it, or immediately pull a fresh replacement from stock with 1 click.

📦 Receive Shipment

When a package arrives from StatLab, MasterTech, or Newcomer. Add the new bottles to Stock and record the new Lot # and Expiry Date.

🛒 Needs Order

Automatically flags when Total ≤ MIN. You can also tap the cart icon to manually flag any reagent your supervisor should order.

3. Checking Special Stain Kits

Stains like Russell Movat Pentachrome require 7 distinct reagents. Click the stain pills (e.g. Movat, Giemsa, Congo Red, Gram, Reticulin) at the top to check at a glance if every required component is in stock and unexpired.

4. Bi-Weekly Audit & CAP Binder Printout

Click "Bi-Weekly Audit Print" anytime to generate a clean, official report formatted exactly to your lab's Histology 205 (v1.0) document. It includes checklist verification boxes for Storage Temp and Expiry Date, plus lines for your initials and supervisor sign-off.
