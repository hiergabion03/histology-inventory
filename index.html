Histology 205 User Guide

Quick cheat-sheet for daily routine, multi-lot tracking, and bi-weekly audits

1. Tracking Specific Lot Numbers (Stock vs. In-Use)

Reagents can have multiple lot numbers simultaneously!
• In-Use (Bench): Shows the exact bottle and lot currently open on the bench.
• Stock (Storage): Shows all unopened bottles split by their exact Lot # and Expiration Date.
• FEFO (First-Expired, First-Out): The app highlights the lot that expires earliest with a badge so you know which one to open next.

2. Your Daily Bench Actions

⚡ Open New Bottle

When you take a sealed bottle to the bench. If you have multiple lots in stock, the app asks which lot you are opening (recommending the earliest expiring one!). Stock drops by 1, In-Use goes up by 1.

✓ Finish Bottle

When an in-use bottle runs dry. You can discard it, or immediately pull a replacement from stock with 1 click.

📦 Receive Shipment / Add Lot

When new bottles arrive from StatLab or Newcomer. Add the bottles with their exact Lot # and Expiration Date.

🛒 Needs Order

Automatically flags when Total ≤ MIN. You can also tap the cart icon to manually flag any reagent your supervisor should order.

3. Bi-Weekly Audit & CAP Binder Printout

Click "Bi-Weekly Audit Print" to generate the exact printable checklist matching your lab's Histology 205 (v1.0) document with multi-lot breakdowns, temperature verification, expiry verification, and supervisor sign-off lines.
